About Me

Study and Practice

My name is Mo Roles Fotso, and I'm a Cameroonian-American originally from Miami, Florida. I have always leaned towards a career in academia, and I have a passion for reading, writing, and theory. I'm particularly interested in the intersections between film theory and archival theory, and the applied practice of the two.

Hobbies and Interests

I consider myself to be a collector of hobbies. I enjoy reading, writing, drawing, singing, photography, and am actively trying to teach myself wood carving and crochet. I also, of course, enjoy watching movies and television, and occasionally writing reviews. My partner and I have a cat and a dog that I enjoy spending quality time with.
