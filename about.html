ABOUT US

The South Florida area is home to people from many different parts of the world, the majority of which come from the Caribbean. As Miami is the largest city in the area, with Miami-Dade county being one of the largest counties in the country, most of the data I will be pulling will be from there, but similar things can be applied to the surrounding areas. Spanish is a first language for a significant number, with 58.5% of Miami-Dade County residents speaking Spanish as a first language and only 27.2% speaking English alone. Haitian Creole, French, Patois, and Portuguese are other common languages found in the area.

As of 2020, 70.2% of people from Miami identify as Hispanic or Latino, and as of 2015 70% of people report speaking Spanish at home. 58% of the population identifies as foreign-born, with a majority coming from the Caribbean and South America. This makes Miami a great place for ESOL speakers, particularly those for whom Spanish is a first language.

That being said, there are still benefits to learning English as well, as the United States as a whole is unfortunately more accessible in general towards those who speak English. It can be overwhelming learning a language, especially when many of the resources available aren’t translatable and can be difficult to navigate.

As such, this site was developed to provide resources for those who are interested in learning the language, and consolidate those resources into one place.
