Mo Roles Fotso

"Everything is temporary"

Artist/Professional Statement

As an aspiring film archivist, I am interested in the temporality of the archive and of film as a medium. My research has centered around the transition from physical to digital film and the way archives are forced to adapt. I am particulary committed to the preservation of marginalized histories, and the caretaking of records and artifacts that are often overlooked.
