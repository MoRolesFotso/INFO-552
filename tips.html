TIPS

Some tips for language learning in general:

  • Consistency is Key: It's important to stay consistent with your practice. The more hours you dedicate and the more frequently the study, the higher your chances for retention. It seems obvious, but it's very important to emphasize!
  • Immersion, Immersion, Immersion: Watch videos in your target language, listen to music in your target language, read books in your target language. It will get easier with time!
  • Peronsalize It!: The process isn't necessarily one-size-fits-all! Switch up your strategies and see what works best for you!
  • Check out online informal communities, such as the language learning subreddit. Get more tips and share your progress!
  • Make It Fun!: You're more likely to stick to a habit of consistent practice if you enjoy it. Make a game of it, or find unique ways to study that stimulate your mind. Don't bore yourself! Have fun with it!
  • Be Patient: It's a slow process, and can be hard or even frustrating at times. Remember that skills take time to learn, and go easy on yourself. With patience and consistency, you'll be fluent before you know it! Enjoy the process and keep on trying!
